## P0: shared preamble

```text
YForge desktop Git client, dense professional tool, design target 1440×900 (must also work at 1280×720). Use the attached YForge design system and its DESIGN brief exactly; the brief's hex values win over generated palettes. Dark theme, lean "Y aurora": near-black backdrop #0B1115 with two very faint soft glows (green #27D17F ~16% upper left, teal #15A0BF ~10% right) as the only gradient; three flat floating panels with 14px corners, 10px gaps, 1px white-12% borders (sidebar and inspector #11181D at 70%, graph #0E151A at 90%); bars have no background and every bar item is a flat control (#0C1217 at 66%, 1px white-7% inset line). Fonts: Geist for UI and the graph, Geist Mono for SHAs, paths, code, and branch names outside the graph. Regions (px): tab bar 40 · command bar 48 · state strip 36 · sidebar 248 · graph fills · inspector 372 · activity bar 30. Rules: status colors always paired with letters (M A D R U !); the commit graph follows GitKraken Desktop's graph topology with YForge's tinted Rail styling (see GRAPH); the state strip is always visible; destructive buttons are danger outline and never the default; sentence case; no uppercase section labels. Never: GitKraken branding, logos, or chrome styling, mascots, illustrations, gradients outside the backdrop glows, glowing or gradient borders and buttons, sheen, cards, emoji, marketing copy, account or plan badges.
```
